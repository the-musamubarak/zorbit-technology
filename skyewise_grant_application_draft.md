## What problem are you solving, who is affected, and why is your solution unique?

Zorbit Technology helps individuals, small businesses, institutions, and growing teams that struggle with scattered information, weak digital visibility, and inefficient manual processes. Many potential clients lack clear dashboards for decision-making, professional websites that build trust, and simple software tools for managing routine operations such as inventory, sales, records, and outstanding debts. This limits their ability to understand performance, reach customers, and grow with confidence.

We solve this by combining **data analysis, data visualization, websites, web applications, and practical business software** in one delivery-focused service. Rather than offering technology for its own sake, Zorbit starts with the client’s real operating challenge and builds an appropriate solution: a clear dashboard, credible website, useful application, or workflow tool. Our approach is unique because it combines analytical thinking with hands-on digital product development, making technology more understandable, accessible, and useful for clients in Nigeria and remotely.

## Briefly detail how you plan to utilize the grant funds

The grant will be used to strengthen Zorbit Technology’s capacity to deliver affordable, high-quality digital solutions to more businesses and individuals. Priority use will include upgrading essential work equipment and licensed software for data analytics, design, and development; improving the company website and digital marketing channels to reach more clients; and supporting core operations such as internet, power, collaboration tools, and project delivery costs.

Part of the grant will also support the development of reusable website, dashboard, and inventory-management solution templates. This will reduce delivery time and help Zorbit offer more accessible technology solutions to small businesses, entrepreneurs, and organisations that need better digital visibility and operational control.
