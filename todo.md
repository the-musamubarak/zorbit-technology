## Source requirements

The attached specification defines Zorbit Technology as a Nigerian CAC-registered technology company based in Abuja. The site should use burnt orange `#E8571A`, deep charcoal `#0F0F0F`, warm white `#FAF7F4`, and amber gold `#F5A623`, with the headline “Turning Data Into Decisions. Building Products That Work.” and the listed services, projects, certifications, stats, contact CTA, and footer information.

## Design decision

Use a premium editorial dark-tech direction: high-contrast charcoal surfaces, burnt-orange signal color, warm-white typography, amber data highlights, asymmetric layouts, fine grid lines, and restrained motion that supports clarity rather than spectacle.

## Style reminders

The build must avoid generic centered layouts, purple gradients, excessive rounded cards, lorem ipsum, and fake testimonials or reviews. Use the requested content verbatim where supplied, and treat unspecified contact details as intentional placeholders only if necessary.
